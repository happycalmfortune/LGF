02 / About

我不是每件事的專家。
但我知道怎麼把事情串起來。

I’m not an expert at everything.
I know how to connect the pieces.

×

我的 corporate 經歷橫跨 FP&A、Product Management、Strategy、Data Analysis 與 AI Adoption。我曾經在大型組織裡，和不同 function 一起處理 business problems。

我也很清楚，在大公司裡很多事情背後都有成熟的 team、data、system 和資源。所以我不會把自己包裝成「一個人可以取代一整個部門」。

我真正想帶出來的能力，是跨部門整合：把問題拆清楚、理解數字、跟不同 stakeholder 對齊、找到 priority，然後讓事情往前走。

現在的實驗我正在建立自己的 one-person practice，同時測試一個問題：一個人配合好的 systems、AI 和工作方法，到底可以做到什麼？

My corporate background spans FP&A, Product Management, Strategy, Data Analysis and AI Adoption. I’ve worked across functions to help teams make decisions and move projects forward.

I also know the limits of corporate experience. Large companies come with teams, systems, data and resources. I’m not trying to position myself as a one-person replacement for an entire department.

What I’m good at is connecting the pieces — framing a problem, understanding the numbers, aligning stakeholders, finding priorities and helping work move forward.

What I’m exploring nowI’m building my own one-person practice and testing a simple question: what can one person accomplish with good systems, AI and a thoughtful way of working?
